📋 How to use this form

  1. Fill in Site Information and Author Information at the top.
  2. Enter the Survey Conditions from your field notes.
  3. Click each Species section to expand it and paste in your field notes — the AI will turn them into formal report language.
  4. Upload your maps and site photos in the Appendix Images section. Add one photo at a time and write a caption for each.
  5. Hit Generate PEA Report — your .docx will download automatically. The first request may take up to 60 seconds.
